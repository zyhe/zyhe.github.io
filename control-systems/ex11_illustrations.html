Closed-loop stability inferred from loop shapes

Task 11.1 · Nyquist plot and the critical point

L(s) = 1(s+1)³  closed with a gain k, negative feedback

Z = N + P. N counts clockwise encirclements of −1/k by the full Nyquist plot, P the open-loop poles in the right half-plane, Z the closed-loop poles there. Stable iff Z = 0.

L(iω) = (1 − 3ω²)/(1+ω²)³ + i (ω³ − 3ω)/(1+ω²)³. It starts at 1 (ω = 0), crosses the imaginary axis at ω = 1/√3, the negative real axis at −1/8 (ω = √3), and ends at the origin with phase −270°.

Task 11.2 · A pole on the D-contour

L₁(s) = 1s (s+1)²

The pole at s = 0 lies on the contour, so the contour must detour around it (segment D4). The detour maps to a huge arc that closes the Nyquist plot. Which side you detour on changes N and P, but never Z.

L₁(iω) = −2/(1+ω²)² + i (ω² − 1)/(ω(1+ω²)²). For ω → 0⁺: Re → −2, Im → −∞. Crosses the real axis at −1/2 (ω = 1). On the detour s = εeiθ: L₁ ≈ e−iθ/ε − 2, an arc of radius 1/ε (here ε = 0.05).

Robustness · gain margin and phase margin

GM: factor by which |kL| may grow before |kL| = 1 at the phase crossover ω180. PM: how far the phase is above −180° at the gain crossover ωc. k scales the magnitude only; the phase curve never moves.

Task 11.3 · Spacecraft with a round-trip delay

G(s) = 1s+1  K(s) = 1TI s  L(s) = K(s)G(s) e−2Tt s

A delay leaves |L(iω)| untouched and subtracts the phase 2Ttω. The Nyquist curve spirals clockwise and eats the phase margin. Stability is lost when the lag at ωc equals the phase margin.

The closed loop from r to the physical output is e−Tts/(e−2Tts + TIs(s+1)). Only the round-trip delay in the denominator matters for stability.
